About the Sable

One of Africa's most prized and visually spectacular trophies, sable are jet black with striking white markings and long, dramatically curved horns that can exceed 160 cm. A bull sable is the pinnacle of plains game hunting.

Hunting Tips

  • They are aggressive when wounded and will charge, so an ethical, well-placed first shot is absolutely essential.
  • Sable prefer dense woodland near water — glass open glades from downwind.
  • Identify mature bulls carefully; females also carry horns and protecting cows is important for herd health.
  • Early morning when bulls emerge to feed is your best window.
