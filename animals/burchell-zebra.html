About the Burchell Zebra

A striking animal with a unique stripe pattern — no two zebras are identical — the Burchell's zebra is commonly found in herds across the open bushveld. They are incredibly tough and social animals that make for a memorable and exciting hunt.

Hunting Tips

  • Zebras are extremely tough and will run enormous distances if poorly hit — use a powerful caliber.
  • Selecting a single animal from a herd requires patience and a clear shooting lane.
  • A heart-lung shot placed just behind the shoulder is most effective.
  • Be ready to shoot quickly — zebra are alert and spook easily, moving fast when alarmed.
