About the Greater Kudu

Known as the 'Grey Ghost of Africa,' the greater kudu is famous for its breathtaking spiral horns — which can exceed 180 cm — and its uncanny ability to vanish into dense bush. A mature bull kudu is one of the most coveted trophies on the continent.

Hunting Tips

  • Early morning and late afternoon are your best windows when kudu are most active.
  • They are extremely cautious and blend masterfully into thorn bush — patience is everything.
  • Glass open clearings and dry riverbeds from a distance rather than pushing into thick cover.
  • A well-placed shoulder shot with a .270 or .30 caliber rifle is the standard approach.
