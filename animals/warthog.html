About the Warthog

A tough and tenacious character of the African bush, the warthog is recognised by its impressive tusks and distinctive facial 'warts.' They are found across the property's open plains and near waterholes, and a large-tusked boar is a prized and fun trophy to hunt.

Hunting Tips

  • Stalking near water sources or setting up at established feeding grounds is the most effective approach.
  • A well-placed shot to the shoulder ensures a quick, ethical kill — warthogs are deceptively tough for their size.
  • Old boars often have the largest tusks; take time to evaluate before shooting.
  • Morning is prime time when warthogs move from their burrows to feed in the open.
