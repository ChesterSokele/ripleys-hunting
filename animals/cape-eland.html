About the Cape Eland

The largest antelope species in the world, the Cape eland is deceptively athletic and can clear two metres despite its massive frame. With spiral horns and a distinctive dewlap, a mature bull eland is a trophy any hunter would be proud of.

Hunting Tips

  • They are slow-moving but can cover great distances when alarmed — track carefully once spooked.
  • Shot placement is key due to their thick skin and substantial bone; a heavy caliber is recommended.
  • Listen for the distinctive clicking of eland tendons — it's a reliable indicator of a nearby herd.
  • Target the heart-lung area behind the shoulder for the cleanest kill.
