About the Steenbok

One of the smallest antelope on the property, the steenbok is a solitary, big-eared grazer found in open grassland and light bush. Despite its size, its keen senses and habit of freezing motionless before bolting make it a genuine test of a hunter's eye and patience.

Hunting Tips

  • Steenbok freeze rather than flee at first — scan open ground carefully before moving through it.
  • They are solitary or found in pairs, never in herds, so each sighting is a single opportunity.
  • A small-caliber rifle such as a .22 centrefire or .243 preserves the small trophy.
  • Glass grassland edges at dawn when steenbok are most active and visible.
