About the Chacma Baboon

Highly intelligent and constantly alert, the chacma baboon is one of the sharpest-eyed animals in the bushveld and often acts as an early-warning system for other game. They move in troops across rocky outcrops and riverine bush, making a mature dog baboon a genuinely challenging trophy.

Hunting Tips

  • Baboon troops post sentries — approach from downwind and stay low to avoid detection.
  • A mature dog baboon is significantly larger than the females; take time to identify the right animal in the troop.
  • They are quick to bolt for rocky crevices or trees — be ready for a fast, decisive shot.
  • A .243 to .308 caliber is well suited, with shot placement to the vitals for a clean, ethical harvest.
