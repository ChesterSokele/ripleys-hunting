About the Red Hartebeest

Known for their long, sloping faces, bracket-shaped horns, and reddish coat, red hartebeests are among the fastest and most enduring antelopes in Africa. They are a classic South African plains game species and a favourite on any safari.

Hunting Tips

  • Hartebeests have exceptional stamina — a poorly placed shot may result in a very long tracking job.
  • Their sloped back means shot placement must compensate for the angled spine; aim well forward behind the shoulder.
  • They are curious by nature and may stop to look back after moving off — use this window wisely.
  • A .270 or .30 caliber is well-suited to the open terrain where hartebeest are typically found.
