About the Blue Wildebeest

Bulkier than their black cousins, the blue wildebeest has a dark grayish-blue coat and a rugged, almost prehistoric appearance. They are one of the most abundant large animals on the African plains and are known for remarkable toughness and group cohesion. A rare golden colour variant is also available on request.

Hunting Tips

  • These animals can absorb a tremendous amount of punishment — a solid, well-placed shoulder shot is crucial.
  • Often found in large, mixed herds; wait for your selected animal to step clear before shooting.
  • A .30 caliber or heavier is recommended to ensure sufficient penetration.
  • Hunt near water sources in the dry season when herds concentrate predictably.
