About the Waterbuck

Recognised by the distinctive white ring around its rump, the waterbuck is a large, robust antelope that thrives near rivers and permanent water sources. The musky smell of its oily coat is unmistakable in the bush. Bulls carry impressive curved horns.

Hunting tips

  1. Waterbuck are known for their exceptionally thick, tough hides — a large-caliber rifle is strongly recommended.
  2. Their preference for dense riverine vegetation makes them difficult to spot and approach.
  3. Set up near water sources at dawn and dusk when bulls come to drink.
  4. A frontal chest shot can be effective when a broadside presentation isn't possible in thick cover.
